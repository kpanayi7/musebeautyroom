Privacy notice

Last updated November 2026

Who we are

Muse Beauty is a lash and brow studio in London N21, run by Ileana Alexandrou. She is responsible for the personal information described here. You can contact her by email at info@musebeautyroom.co.uk or on Instagram @musebeauty_room_.

This website

This website has no forms, no tracking and no advertising cookies. The fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your device's IP address when the page loads.

Booking

When you book, the booking page is run by Timely. They collect your name, contact details and appointment details so we can confirm your booking and send you reminders. Timely's own privacy policy explains how they handle that information.

At your appointment

If you tell us about allergies, skin conditions or sensitivities, for example at your patch test, we use that only to keep you safe during your treatment. After you book, we ask you to fill in a short consultation form, and we keep it with your client record.

Birthday offers and news

Your consultation form asks for your gender and date of birth. Both are optional: you can choose "Prefer not to say" and leave your birthday blank. If you also tick the box to hear from us, we'll use your date of birth to email you a birthday offer, and occasionally send news by email. We only send these emails if you've ticked that box. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also message us to stop.

Photos

We only share before-and-after photos of your treatment on our website or social media if you say yes on your consultation form. You can change your mind at any time by messaging us, and we'll stop using them.

Payments

Card payments are processed by Revolut. We don't see or store your full card number.

How long we keep it

We keep your client record and consultation form for up to 3 years after your last appointment, then delete them. Payment records are kept for 6 years, because the law requires it for tax.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, to correct it or to have it deleted. Email info@musebeautyroom.co.uk and we'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how your information is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
