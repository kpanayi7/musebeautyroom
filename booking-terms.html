Booking terms

Last updated November 2026

Booking

You can book any treatment online through our booking page, which is run by Timely. Your booking is confirmed when you receive a confirmation email. The exact studio address is in that confirmation.

After you book, we'll ask you to fill in a short consultation form so we know about any allergies or skin conditions before your appointment.

Patch test

Before your first lash lift, brow lamination or tint, you need a free patch test at least 48 hours before your appointment. It makes sure the products suit your skin. We can't carry out these treatments without one.

Pregnancy

If you're pregnant or breastfeeding, please tell us when you book and on your consultation form. Hormone changes can make skin more sensitive and results less predictable, so we may suggest a new patch test, or waiting for some treatments.

Before a wax

Please stop using any active skincare products, such as retinol or exfoliating acids, for at least 5 days before your wax appointment.

Cancelling or moving an appointment

Please give at least 48 hours' notice if you need to cancel or move your appointment, so the time can be offered to someone else. You can manage your booking from your confirmation email.

A deposit is taken when you book. If you cancel or move with less than 48 hours' notice, or don't turn up, you lose your deposit. With at least 48 hours' notice, you keep it.

Payment

Prices are shown on our website and booking page in pounds sterling. You pay a deposit when you book, and the rest at your appointment by card, cash or bank transfer.

Refunds

If you're not happy with your treatment, please message us within 72 hours with a photo. We'll offer you a free correction appointment, normally within 7 days. If a correction isn't possible, we'll offer a partial or full refund instead. Results that change because aftercare wasn't followed aren't covered. This doesn't affect your legal rights.

Gift vouchers

Gift vouchers can be bought online for any amount and arrive by email. They can be used towards any treatment and are valid for 1 year from the date of purchase.

Contact

For anything about your booking, message us on Instagram @musebeauty_room_.
